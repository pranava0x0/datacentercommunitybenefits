# Data-center accounts to follow (Instagram and social)

A lead list for the curators. Each entry is a discovery source, not a citation:
a fact from a post ships only after its primary source (a council record,
filing or news article) is fetched and probe-verified. Accounts that are
people's own names are deliberately excluded.

handles below come from public web search. Each is marked verified once
