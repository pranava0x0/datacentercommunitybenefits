# Moratorium geography and DOE references

2026-11-06. Approved scope: Moratoriums → Map, full public laboratory / EM
footprint with announcements highlighted. Elections uses a summary table with
winner / actual prior officeholder and sourced policy topics; full quotations
open on demand.

The map uses generalized Census state, county, incorporated-place and government
township boundaries. A boundary identifies the jurisdiction associated with a
record; it does not establish parcel-level legal applicability. Individual host
agreements and company plans do not fill whole jurisdictions. Default status is
recorded enacted / in effect, excluding explicitly lifted moratoriums. Proposed
and all-recorded filters remain available.

DOE locations are published reference coordinates, including field offices,
with coordinate-source links and location kinds. They are not development
parcels. All 17 laboratory organizations and all 16 EM map entries are covered
by 33 distinct points after shared lab/EM references are merged. Named satellite
and other asset gaps remain in the source coverage section. Six dated DOE
announcements retain their source status, including lease negotiations and
pending power-service approval. National announcements without verified local
sites do not acquire guessed pins.

343 eligible legal records map to 288 boundaries (including state backgrounds).
Springhill Township PA remains unmatched because its county is unverified.
Baltimore County is distinct from Baltimore city; Augusta uses the consolidated
municipal place, not Augusta CCD. Census shapes are references, not legal maps.

Native SVG, no external map service, tiles or new dependencies. Map geometry,
DOE data and module load only on the Map sub-tab. Search includes announcement
text and associated sites; state/status filters, zoom, pan, AK/HI shortcuts,
keyboard markers and a text location list link back to canonical legal records.
Secondary filters sit below the map so phone users can see it sooner.

Original file/native previews use generated classic-script adapters when
JSON/ES-module loading is unavailable. Hosted production keeps ordinary JSON
and module requests. Adapters derive from the canonical output and source
modules; they are not a second curated dataset.
