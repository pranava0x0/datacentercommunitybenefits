# International expansion: research synthesis

Inputs: europe.json, apac.json, americas_mea_global.json (Haiku agents; quotes came through a summarizing fetch). Nothing below was re-fetched by me. "Fetched" means an agent reported fetching the page; "lead" means search-snippet only or an agent-flagged gap. Every quote must be re-verified against the primary page before it ships (the v1.19 rule applies doubly with translation in the loop).

Note: URLs ending in "..." were truncated in my skim; take the full URL from the matching source_url in europe.json / apac.json / americas_mea_global.json.

Legend: **[F]** fetched by an agent, **[S]** secondary source only (trade press / law-firm note), **[L]** lead, unverified.

## 0. What the current model already assumes

- `Project` already has `country` (default "US") and its `state` field is documented "two-letter US state code (or country code)". So sites are the least-US-locked record. `claimed_investment_usd` and `power_mw` are the only hard-wired units.
- `Moratorium` (`jurisdiction_type` city|county|state|federal, `state_code` validator-required for non-federal), `RateCase.state_code` (federal must be "US"), `Policy.state_code` (required for state/county/city scope), `Tariff.jurisdiction_level` state|federal, `Signatory` (US pledge), `COMPANY_SLUGS` (15 slugs) and app.js `STATE_NAMES` / 50-state strip / `#state/XX` panel are all US-only.
- 8 tabs; first paint is `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, `companies.json`, `home.json` = 142 KB, 5 requests.

### 1.2 Europe (country level)

- **Ireland.** The strongest US-analog story: a grid-capacity "moratorium" that is really a regulator connection policy.
  - CRU Large Energy User connection policy, proposed decision CRU/202504 (2025-02-18): [S] https://www.matheson.com/insights/large-energy-users-connection-policy-update/ . Final decision December 2025: [S] https://www.dcbyte.com/news-blogs/ireland-reopens-the-grid-for-data-centres/ — applies only to new connection applications, requires on-site/nearby generation or storage, and an 80% domestic-renewable match (per DCByte summary; primary CRU decision not fetched).
  - Maps to **Tariff/RateCase-adjacent** (regulator decision on who bears grid cost and what a load must bring), not Moratorium. Regulator is CRU, not a PUC; capacity in **MVA**.
  - CSO metered data: data centres 7,663 GWh in 2025, 23% of metered electricity [S] https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/07/07/data-centres-account-for-almost-one-quarter (primary CSO table is the better citation).
  - Leads: effective connection freeze since 2021 [L] (Irish Examiner); Dublin City Council 12-month pause [L]; Microsoft Grange Castle, Google sites [L].
- **Netherlands.** Siting is municipal/provincial, congestion is TSO (TenneT). (URL truncated in the source file; re-pull). Meta Zeewolde dropped 2022 [F] https://dutchnews.nl/2022/07/not-a-good-match-facebook-parent-drops-zeewolde-data-centre-p... (truncated). The heat-to-homes condition is the cleanest **community-benefit instrument** found in Europe.

### 1.3 Asia-Pacific

- **Singapore.** Best-documented formal regime in the whole set. 2019-2022 de facto pause [S] https://www.reedsmith.com/articles/singapores-data-centre-expansion-jurong-island-sus... ; lifted 2022, pilot DC-CFA (about 80 MW to four operators, 2023); Green Data Centre Roadmap 2024; DC-CFA2 launched 2025-12-01, 200 MW provisionally allocated 2026-08-21, 50 MW each to Digital Realty, Equinix, Keppel DC, ST Telemedia, with a >50% green-energy condition [S] https://w.media/?p=42718 ; primary EDB PDF exists but was not fetched. Maps: a **competitive allocation**, which the schema cannot hold (MW per operator per round, not per site). Nearest pledge analog (a published, enforceable bid commitment) but it is a green-energy condition, not community benefit. Hyperscaler spend figures (AWS $9bn) are BMI-via-TNGlobal [S].
- **Malaysia (Johor).** Tier 1/2 approvals halted Nov 2025 on water grounds; reclaimed-water requirement [F] https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2025/11/1324188/johor-tightens-approvals-data-... ; [S] https://w.media/johor-stops-approving-tier-1-and-tier-2-data-centers-due-to-water-ove... . The only **true state-level approval halt** with a stated reason, so it fits Moratorium best (jurisdiction = Malaysian state, trigger = water). Tariff is federal (RP4), water is a state utility.
- **Japan.** National decentralisation push (METI Watt-Bit, GX zones, cluster subsidies); grid waits up to 10 years [S] https://shulman-advisory.com/japans-data-center-build-out-bottlenecks-and-strategic-d... . Subsidy paid to municipalities. Japanese primaries not read.
- **India.** No national policy yet (preparing) [S] https://inc42.com/buzz/centre-to-roll-out-data-centre-policy-amid-power-cost-concerns ; Karnataka reviewing its policy (water) [S] https://thesouthfirst.com/karnataka/explained-why-karnataka-is-reviewing-its-data-cen... ; Google Visakhapatnam 1 GW, $15bn vs a nearly $10bn agreement value, a figure conflict in one article [S] https://thesouthfirst.com/andhrapradesh/googles-largest-ai-hub-outside-us-coming-up-i... . "Benefit" is usually a state incentive package (land, power tariff, stamp duty). English-language, so the best-fit non-US ingestion language after Anglosphere. Thane/Amazon protest [L].
- **Australia.** NSW Data Centre Policy Framework (2026-08-17) and proposed 5 MW grid-access bill [S] https://www.claytonutz.com/insights/2026/august/nsw-draws-the-line-what-the-new-data-... ; proposed federal national-cabinet rules (own connection costs, water, siting) [S] https://aiunderstanding.org/news/australia-s-proposed-ai-data-centre-rules-could-spur... — conceptually the **closest cousin to the US Ratepayer Pledge** (developer pays connection). Project Mars (Goodman, 90 MW, Lane Cove NSW) withdrawn 2026-09-29 [S] https://ia.acs.org.au/article/2026/controversial-sydney-data-centre-plan-scrapped.htm . Stargate Australia/NextDC S7 [L]. English-language, state-level planning (State Significant Development register is a possible source).
- **Indonesia (Batam).** Water protests Aug-Oct 2026; officials told firms to desalinate; informal slowdown, no moratorium [S] https://thenextweb.com/news/batam-data-centres-water-protest . Good CommunityResponse material; not a formal instrument.
- **South Korea.** Seoul district mayors back architectural review (2026-08-12) [S] https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026081307284684694 ; Special Act on Distributed Energy (effective 2024-06) makes Seoul-adjacent siting costlier [S] https://www.datacentres.com/news/seoul-s-grid-lockout-how-korea-s-decentralization-la... . Korean primaries unread.
- **Taiwan.** One failed search. A gap, not a finding.

### 1.5 Pattern across regions (editorially useful)

1. Grid connection is the binding constraint almost everywhere; instruments are **allocation/queue/cost rules**, not bans (Ireland, Singapore, Seoul, UK, Japan, Ile-de-France). The US "moratorium" frame covers few of them.
2. **Water** triggers halts in Johor, Batam, Chile, Uruguay, Queretaro, Aragon; **power** triggers them in Ireland, Netherlands, Singapore.
3. The "blueprint of solutions" the owner wants lives in: **mandated/negotiated heat reuse** (Amsterdam, EnEfG, Odense, Narvik), **entry conditions on new load** (Ireland 80% renewables match; Singapore >50% green; Johor reclaimed water), and **developer pays connection** (Australia federal proposal, Quebec tariff).
4. Direct hyperscaler community-benefit instruments (funds, host agreements, local hiring) are nearly absent in what the agents fetched. Expect the international dataset to be policy-heavy and Claim-light.

## 2. Schema gaps

1. **Jurisdiction model.** Introduce `country` (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2) as a first-class field on Moratorium, Policy, Tariff, RateCase, Signatory-like records (Project has it). Replace `state_code` with `country` + `subnational_code` (ISO 3166-2, e.g. `AU-NSW`, `MY-01`, `CA-QC`, `ES-AR`) + free-text `locality`. Keep `state_code` as a derived/alias for `country == "US"` so the 102+ existing records and the `#state/XX` panel don't churn. Widen `jurisdiction_type` to include `region`/`province`/`municipality`/`supranational`; `federal` becomes `national`. Existing validators ("federal must be US", "state needs state_code") need country-aware rewrites. Special cases found: Singapore (city-state, no subnational), Batam (free-trade zone authority BP Batam), Seoul two-tier proposer/adopter, EU (supranational).
2. **Currency and units.** `claimed_investment_usd` and `value_usd` are USD. Needed: `{amount, currency (ISO 4217), as_stated: bool, fx_rate_date?}` with USD derived and *dated*, never stored over the original. Examples that conflict: Visakhapatnam $15bn vs ~$10bn/Rs 83,000 crore, Hydro-Quebec 13 CAD cents/kWh. Power: `power_mw` plus an `import_capacity_mva` (Ireland's tiers are MVA); do not convert silently.
3. **Language and translation.** `Claim.statement` is verbatim. Add `original_language` (BCP-47), and for non-English: `statement` stays in the original, `statement_en` + `translation_by` (`human|machine`) + `translation_verified: bool`. UI shows the original with a "translated" label. Policy `key_terms` need the same. This directly serves the rule "quote as published".
4. **Regulators other than PUCs.** `Tariff`/`RateCase` carry `utility`, `docket_number`, PUC semantics. Add `regulator` (name + country) and `proceeding_type`. Quebec's Régie (R-4333-2026) and Ireland's CRU (CRU/202504 is a decision number, not a docket) both fit once the regulator is a field. TSO queue policies (RTE, TenneT, EirGrid) are not regulator proceedings; decide whether they are Policy `regulation` or a new `grid_policy` instrument value.
5. **EU-level instruments across countries.** A `Policy` with `scope: supranational` and `applies_to_countries: [ISO2...]` (or "EU-27"), plus `transposed_by` links to national acts (EED -> German EnEfG). Needed so the country page can show "EU rules that apply here" without duplicating the record 27 times.
6. **Instrument vocabulary gaps.** (a) Competitive MW allocation round (Singapore DC-CFA/CFA2): needs `allocation_round` and per-operator MW. (b) Grid-connection/queue policy (CRU, Ile-de-France). (c) Designation scheme (UK AI Growth Zones, Aragon PIGA). (d) Subsidy/tax regime on the *giving* side (Brazil Redata, Japan cluster subsidies, Saudi electricity subsidy). (e) Court ruling (Chile, Uruguay) — currently only `SiteUpdate.kind=lawsuit`; fine for sites but not standalone. (f) Reporting mandate (EED, EnEfG PUE). Use `Policy.instrument` extensions, not a new tab.
7. **Moratorium reason taxonomy.** `MoratoriumReasonType` is energy|water|air_quality|noise|transparency|equity; fits. But a status like `de facto freeze` / `informal` (Singapore 2019-22, Batam, Ireland 2021-25) needs a distinct marker so a pause isn't presented as a legal instrument (Loudoun lesson).
8. **Data withheld / disputed disclosure** (Uruguay, Chile, Queretaro): a boolean or `disclosure_status` on Project water facts. Optional.
9. **Registries as connectors.** Candidates, ranked: EU EED database (aggregated, benchmark only, check licence/format); Irish CSO metered statistics (open, periodic); NSW Planning Portal SSD register; Regie de l'energie docket system (structured URLs, French); UK planning portals (per-council, unwieldy); UK NESO/Ofgem queue (lead). None verified for licence or machine access by the agents. Per project rule, curated first, connector only where URLs are stable.
10. **Pledge analogs.** There is no multi-company signed pledge outside the US in the research. Analogs by strength: Singapore DC-CFA2 awardees (binding bid condition), UK AI Growth Zones (government designation), Australia federal proposed rules (not in force), C40 pact (mayors, not companies). Recommendation: **do not extend `Signatory`**; show these as Policy records and leave the Pledge tab US-only, titled as such.
11. **Company scoping.** Tracked 15 slugs appear internationally (Meta Odense/Zeewolde, Google Cerrillos/Teros/Visakhapatnam, Microsoft Queretaro/Olkaria, OpenAI Stargate Norway/UAE/Australia, xAI Saudi, AWS Singapore/Japan). Local operators dominate the instruments (Nscale, NextDC, Goodman, Khazna/G42, Keppel, ST Telemedia, Teraco, Equinix, Digital Realty, HUMAIN, Aker). CLAUDE.md's two-gate test (>=1 GW and own community-impact framing) would admit very few. Recommend: sites by non-tracked operators are allowed as Projects with `company_slug` null/"other" + `operator_name` string, **without** creating Company records, mirroring the Signatory "thin tier" logic. Whether to relax the Company rule is an owner decision. Existing Equinix/Digital Realty "out of scope" line also needs re-reading, since they are Singapore DC-CFA2 awardees.
12. **Theme taxonomy.** The frozen 8 mostly fit, but heat reuse (district heating) has no home (closest: `energy` or `infrastructure`), and "grid cost allocation" has no home (`energy`). Per CLAUDE.md a 9th theme is a migration; recommend mapping to existing themes with a tag rather than adding one.
13. **Ratepayer tab.** Its premise (pledge + signatory dates) doesn't generalise. Keep US-only.

## 4. Risks

- **Accuracy.** (a) Most facts above are [S]. Agents saw summaries; "verbatim" is approximate. (b) Headline-vs-action: Chile "partially revokes" vs a court-ordered revision; Ireland "moratorium" vs a connection policy; Singapore "moratorium" vs administrative freeze; Johor "ban" is a tier-based approval halt per Mezha/Reuters via secondary [L]. Apply the v1.19/Loudoun rule: find the jurisdiction's own statement. (c) Number conflicts inside single articles (India $15bn vs $10bn; Singapore capacity). (d) Law numbers from search synthesis (Brazil 15.504/2026; Quebec Decree 89-2026) may be wrong; fetch the gazette text. (e) Translation: machine-translated quotes treated as verbatim would break the core editorial rule.
- **Scope creep.** International multiplies jurisdictions (150+ plausible subnational units) and breaks the "50-state strip" honesty model. Without a bounded country list, the dataset becomes unreviewable. The 2026-07 poisoned bulk moratorium batch (fabricated bill records, per CLAUDE.md) is a live risk with foreign-language bulk lists.
- **Performance budget.** First paint is 142 KB / 5 requests with a 275 KB cap. Do not preload any international payload; one lazy `international.json` (or per-country) fetched on tab/filter activation; map stays Explorer-only. A country selector on existing tabs would force loading international records into each existing lazy payload and could bloat them, so prefer separate payloads joined at render. The "totals" block in `coverage.json` and `home.json` digest would need a country dimension without growing noticeably.
- **Editorial framing.** The blueprint orientation is served by heat reuse, entry conditions and cost-allocation rules. But critical content (Aragon, Batam, Queretaro) is mostly community response, and keeping it while the benefit side is thin could skew the tone; the Pledge tab's absence abroad may read as "nobody does this". Never compare country "scores"; no trust index.
- **Maintenance load.** The daily refresh routine runs 20 minutes on Sonnet with a queue derived from `STATE_NAMES`. Adding countries multiplies units; foreign-language sources, bot walls (DCD returns 403 repeatedly) and unreliable fetching mean more parked/BLOCKED units. Recommend country-level queue units at a longer interval (e.g. 120 days) and excluding translation-dependent units from unattended runs.
- **Legal/IP.** Quoting foreign press/regulator text: same limits as US; note licence terms of EU database before redistributing any of it.
