# Framework and moratorium field refresh — November 6, 2026

The work keeps the two existing directories. Frameworks describes development
rules, agreements and company plans; Moratoriums describes pauses and bans.
A shared instrument may link between them. Existing Raleigh zoning and
Loudoun special-exception rules are distinct from their temporary pause records.

Options considered: keeping numbered clusters would obscure individual sites;
a separate map page would add navigation; individual DOE stars in the existing
map preserve coordinates and direct access. The third option is implemented.

the city's FAQ. All primary URLs and supporting resources are in seed records.

and two further Raleigh pages. No vote, formal ordinance number or enactment
date was inferred from a headline or capture date.

Missing dates and reasons remain explicit in the interface. Company plans
need not have bill numbers; proposed or failed bills need not have enactment
dates; an MW threshold is not assigned where another coverage test also applies.

## Map and controls

33 individually positioned stars replace numbered clusters. Announcement-linked
sites retain a contrasting fill. Jurisdictions and stars have hover/focus
previews and keyboard activation; selection exposes canonical records or DOE
sources. The full-width map precedes selection; the detail pane can be closed.
Colors, combined layers, dashed proposed outlines and dotted inactive outlines
have literal legend entries. The caption distinguishes unshaded map coverage
from an assertion that a locality has no policy.

Primary filters stay visible with labels. Extra scope/principle filters open
in a disclosure and reveal selected URL filters. The benefits checkbox remains
a primary control. Tablet navigation uses the same five-column arrangement as
phone navigation. No dependency or data-model change.

Remaining field work is recorded in BACKLOG.md: 17 framework dates and
86 moratorium reason lists remain absent, including legitimate unknown dates.
This pass does not claim to have resolved every absent optional field.
